# The Intelligent Organization — Readiness Review

A read of every live document in this folder against the source of truth
([What it is](../product/intelligent-org-features.md)) and against each
other, with one question: **is the record coherent and complete enough to
start building, and if not, what has to exist first?**

Short answer: **coherent, nearly complete, and not yet cleared to build.**
The product, protocol, agent, and plan documents agree with each other on
every rule that matters — roles, the two laws, the proposal life, the work
tree, who signs what. The drift found is small and is fixed in place or
listed below as a decision. What was missing was not more design; it was
three things the documents themselves say must come first and nobody had
done:

1. **The code verification** that [Current State § What to verify](../architecture/intelligent-org-current-state.md#what-to-verify-before-building)
   demands before step 1 — now
   [Codebase verification](../architecture/intelligent-org-codebase-verification.md).
2. **A map from the prototype to the desktop and to the evaluation
   fixtures** — the prototype is 14 000 lines that Phase 0 says the `org`
   feature "grows from" and the evaluation plan says the seeds are "drawn
   from", with no document saying which parts — now
   [Prototype map](../product/intelligent-org-prototype-map.md).
3. **One development plan.** Four build orders exist (Design § Build order,
   Phase 0 § First things, Org agent § 20, AI evaluation § Order of work)
   and they agree, but none is sliced into pull requests with dependencies,
   owners, tests, and gates across relay, desktop, CLI, and agent — now
   [Development plan](./intelligent-org-development-plan.md).

This document is the record of the review: what was checked, what drifted,
what is fixed, what must still be decided, and the gate to start.
